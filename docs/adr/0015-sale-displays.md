# Sale Displays replace the auto Preset gallery

In-world cosmetic merchandising uses admin-placed **Sale Displays** that bind a
**Published Catalog Entry**, not movable Preset showcases and not a parallel product
catalog. The Shaper’s auto-laid “one showcase per Preset” gallery is retired; The Shaper
becomes a floor of curated Sale Displays. Admins place, select, move, and remove Sale
Displays via **Build → Buildings → Sale Display** like other building placeables (obstacle
foot), and may use full Build in The Shaper (admin-only; players stay locked out). Unbound
displays are admin-only; players only see bound Published entries (slot-aware mannequin or
floor deployable) and buy via the existing Cosmetic Unlock path. Kiosks outside The Shaper
are allowed under the same rules; Wardrobe Shop remains.

## Considered Options

- **Keep auto Preset showcases; add buyable pedestals beside them** — rejected: two layouts
  and browse-vs-buy models in one room.
- **Bind a Preset (price elsewhere)** — rejected: players buy Catalog Entries; Presets have
  no SKU/price/publish state.
- **Campaign-tab / overlay-only place (no Build menu)** — rejected in favor of Buildings-menu
  placeables that select and move like blocks.
- **Sale Display–only Build strip in The Shaper (no general Building)** — superseded: operators
  want admins to layout The Shaper with full Build like other rooms; players remain non-builders.
- **Shaper-only placement** — rejected for v1 scope: operators wanted Hub/Commons kiosks too;
  unbound-admin-only keeps empty pedestals out of player view.
