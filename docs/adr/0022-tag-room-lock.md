# Participants cannot leave the room during Tag Countdown and Tag Round

A Tag Round is a chase in the room everyone already occupies. Letting a Participant Enter a Teleporter, open Rooms, or Return to Hub mid-round is an escape hatch: dump the Mosquito by warping away, or quit without disconnecting. Tag Call waiting still allows Leave and room change. Disconnect still drops a Participant (the party continues). The 5s result linger lifts the lock so people can walk off after the chase.
