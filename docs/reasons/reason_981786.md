# reason_981786 — Tutorial faucet payout priority

## Why THE-LARGER-SYSTEM changed

We agreed that new Nimiq Pay learners must see their tutorial faucet NIM leave the hot wallet
quickly even when the outgoing queue holds thousands of normal mining/goal jobs. That is a
durable first-contact product rule, not a one-off ops tweak: without it, the lesson "NIM arrives
fast" fails under load even though the UI already acks optimistically.

## How it serves the larger system

- Keeps **first-contact** experience independent of **bulk reward backlog** scale.
- Uses an explicit Pay-Intent `priority` field (not claim-id sniffing), so producers stay honest
  and the Payout Service / Outbox share one contract.
- Bounds the special case: strict priority for ready jobs, auto-bulk exclusion, flush/admin bulk
  still clear leftover priority jobs - no second fairness scheduler forever.
