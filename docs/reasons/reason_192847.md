# Reason 192847 — Path Playback heartbeat and welcome orders

## Why `THE-LARGER-SYSTEM.md` was updated

The occupied-room never-rewind contract (reason 847293) left late joiners without an in-flight path and left missed `moveOrder` / `moveAbort` with no recovery except waiting for drain. The recorded "today" now includes `walkId`, joiner `welcome.moveOrders`, and a ~1 Hz analytic `poseHeartbeat` so avatars stay visually in sync without restoring tick pose streaming.

## How this supports the larger system

- **Coherence** — One pose source (analytic) and one playback clock (`serverNowMs`) for orders, welcome, and heartbeat. Never-rewind still forbids snap-back; implicit abort (`walking=false` or a greater `walkId`) is the named exception.
- **Evolvability** — Packet-loss recovery is a duplicate + heartbeat, not a higher `STATE_BROADCAST_MIN_MS` pose rate.
- **Direction** — Poor connections may look slightly late. Snap-back along a walk remains the defect.
