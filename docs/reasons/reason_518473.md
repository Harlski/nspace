# reason_518473 — Tag Round stays in the room for the people playing it

**Why:** "Play stays in the current room" was only "the game does not teleport you onto a Match Pitch." Participants could still Enter a Teleporter or change Room, which dumps the Mosquito and starves Portrait Play of nearby targets. Locking room exit for Participants during Tag Countdown and Tag Round makes the in-room sport actually in-room. Off-screen Participant Edge Markers are the same fairness idea from the other direction: a narrow viewport should still know where the other Participants are.

**How it serves the larger system:** In-room mini-games keep Hub/Commons movement and the shared floor, but party lock at Start has to mean the party stays in that floor. Isolation (Match Pitch) is still the tool for sports that leave the room; Tag does not grow a second escape hatch. Disconnect remains the honest quit. Client-only edge chevrons do not change server authority.
