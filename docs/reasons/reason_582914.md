# Reason 582914

**Date:** 2026-11-21

**Why `THE-LARGER-SYSTEM` changed:** Operator Telegram was firing on every world entry (the old Connect Notice). That channel is how a small team notices who is in the world, and a message per door crossing buried the useful pattern: who actually played, for how long, and how much NIM they earned. The hourly digest is the durable shape of that signal.

**How this serves the larger system:** One scheduled read on the Analytics Service keeps Event Log scans off the game tick (same rule as overview and daily stats) and keeps Telegram delivery on the game. Gameplay NIM is counted when it is credited, so the table stays meaningful between end-of-day payout flushes. Future operator metrics belong as another column on this digest, not as another per-event ping.
