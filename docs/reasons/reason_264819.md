# Why THE-LARGER-SYSTEM was updated

Login streak used to credit only `POST /api/auth/verify`. A 12-hour JWT lets a player enter the world the next UTC morning without signing again, so a real in-world day was skipped and the next verify reset the counter. Recording that a streak day is **wallet presence that UTC day** (room enter or verify) keeps the product rule aligned with what players mean by signing in.

This supports coherent long-lived player expectations: consecutive days in the space should count, without forcing a Hub/Pay re-sign every calendar day.
