# Reason 847293 — Presence without full-roster pose

## Why `THE-LARGER-SYSTEM.md` was updated

Occupied rooms were sending a full-roster `state` (including live walker poses) for typing, tab-away / NIM-send, Challenge flags, and similar cues. That was both an O(N^2) bandwidth tax and the trigger for Path Playback snap-back: after a walk drained, the next presence snapshot could own `targetPos` from a lagged origin. The contract is now recorded so new social flags cannot reintroduce `broadcastRoomStateFull`, and lag is not "fixed" by restoring tick pose streaming.

## How this supports the larger system

- **Coherence** — Locomotion stays on `moveOrder` / `moveAbort`; presence stays a one-player `stateDelta`. Clients share one never-rewind rule instead of ad hoc `syncState` exceptions.
- **Evolvability** — New typing-like cues have a named pattern (subject delta, omit pose while a grid path is in flight) instead of copying a full-roster helper.
- **Direction** — Poor connections may look slightly late. Snap-back along a walk is the defect. Heartbeat, compact paths, and spatial interest remain later slices.
