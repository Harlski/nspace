# reason_739261 — Tag Round Pay Zoom

**Why:** Nimiq Pay portrait sees a tall, narrow slice of the room. Tag Room Lock keeps Participants on that floor; Participant Edge Markers tell them where the others are. They still cannot see as much floor as a desktop player, so a chase in Hub/Commons is harder to read. Forcing Telescope range for Pay Participants during Tag Countdown and Tag Round is the same fairness idea: extra viewing for the host that needs it, then restore. It is not an unlock of Telescope as a feature (no hold, no achievement).

**How it serves the larger system:** In-room mini-games stay on the shared floor and keep Path Playback. Camera assists that do not change authority can be client-only and host-specific. Reusing the Telescope frustum (not a new zoom mode) keeps one "how far can you see" number. Hub/desktop play is unchanged.
