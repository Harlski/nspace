# Public patch notes — players (`0.7.1`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [FIX] When you have used today’s Daily Earn Allowance, you can no longer mine gold blocks for 0 NIM (they stay available for players who still have room). Trying to mine opens a short message with a path into Achievements.
- [NEW] After a successful mine under a daily ceiling, a quick pulse shows how much NIM you have left today (for example `5 / 20 NIM remaining`).
- [NEW] **You Kaan Do It** — Social achievement for logging in on 100 consecutive UTC days (150 Achievement Points).
