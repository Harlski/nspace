# Public patch notes — players (`0.7.7`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- **[NEW]** The connect screen shows today’s visitors as identicons hovering around the login card (same faces as in-game), with light motion and occasional emotes.
- Quiet days stay quiet — only real joins from shared spaces that day appear (private invite rooms do not).
- The cast is decoration only; tap the usual sign-in controls to enter.
- **[FIX]** After you finish a walk, the camera no longer snaps back along the path when a late network update arrives. Starting the same walk again under lag also no longer restarts you at the path origin.
