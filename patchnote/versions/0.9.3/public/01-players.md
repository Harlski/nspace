# Public patch notes — players (`0.9.3`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [NEW] Public Rooms, from the Commons west door or room select, is a hallway of teleporters to public player rooms. The most popular rooms sit closest to the entrance. Each teleporter shows the room name, the owner's face, and how many people are inside.
- [FIX] If you already have a username, sign-in opens the Hub. It no longer reloads the loading screen over and over.
