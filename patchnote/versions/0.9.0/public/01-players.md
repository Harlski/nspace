# Public patch notes — players (`0.9.0`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- Nothing changes for mining until an admin turns the check on.
- [NEW] If mining is disabled on your account, the claim bar says so and shows a check you can complete.
- [NEW] Completing the check turns mining back on for that wallet. You can still walk and chat while it is disabled.
- [CHANGE] If the check is not completed in time, mining stays restricted until an admin allows it again.
- The one-time tutorial mine does not start this. Guests still cannot mine.
