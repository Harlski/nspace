Sign in on a shared computer by scanning a QR code with Nimiq Pay or Nimiq Wallet on your phone. After your wallet signs, the phone shows the same four-character code as the computer, and only **Yes, sign in** lets that computer in. **Cancel** leaves it signed out. Your wallet password stays on the phone.
