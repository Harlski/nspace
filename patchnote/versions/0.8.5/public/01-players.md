# Public patch notes — players (`0.8.5`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [NEW] On the Main Menu in a browser, choose **Sign in with QR**. Inside Nimiq Pay that button stays hidden.
- [NEW] Pick **Nimiq Pay** or **Nimiq Wallet**, then scan. The address under the code has a copy button for when Pay will not open a link that includes a port.
- [NEW] The computer shows a four-character code, such as T3XR. After your wallet signs, the phone asks **Sign in on T3XR?** with your identicon, your username when this account has one, and a small faded address.
- [NEW] **Yes, sign in** lets the other computer in. **Cancel** leaves it signed out and returns the phone to the sign-in page.
- After you approve, the phone stays on a light signed-in card, including if the page reloads. The computer enters on its own.
- On a shared computer, the machine never asks for your wallet password.
