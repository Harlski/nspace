# Public patch notes — players (`0.8.2`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [NEW] **Mosquito Tag** on the Games Wheel (mosquito). Tap to gather people where you stand. Nearby players Join from the bubble, the HUD check, or the other player's menu. Start when at least one other person has joined (up to eight including you). After a short countdown you can still walk through, someone random gets the mosquito. Walk onto or beside another player in the party to pass it. Green boost pillars give the holder a short speed burst. After 60 seconds, whoever still has it is Stung and walks slower for a bit. Watchers stay in the same room. No teleport, no NIM. Not on soccer pitches, Free Play Field, Tutorial, Pixel, or Canvas. You cannot mix it with a soccer Challenge.
- [CHANGE] **Free Play Field scores on mobile.** The country ranking no longer stacks down the screen. You see today's leader as `1. {flag} {goals}`. Tap that chip to open the full ranking, including yesterday's champion. The chip is the high score only; pick-your-country stays on your profile (and on the desktop scoreboard). On desktop the panel still expands and collapses in place.
