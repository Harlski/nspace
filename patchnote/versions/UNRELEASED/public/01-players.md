# Public patch notes — players (`UNRELEASED`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- **[NEW]** **Username required** — the next time you sign in with a wallet and you do not have a username yet, you choose one before you can enter. **Not now** no longer lets you in. This includes the Nimiq Pay tutorial. If a moderator has banned you from setting a name, you can still enter. If a moderator clears your name, you choose a new one on your next sign-in.
- **[CHANGE]** Guest visits still use the invite nickname. You do not pick a wallet username for those.
