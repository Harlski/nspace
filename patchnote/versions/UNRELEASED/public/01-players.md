---

- On the Main Menu (browser / desktop), choose **Sign in with Nimiq Pay QR**.
- Pick **Nimiq Pay** or **Nimiq Wallet**, then scan the code (the link is shown under it). Pay opens the confirm flow inside Nimiq Pay; Wallet opens the sign-in page so you can confirm with Nimiq Wallet. After you sign, the phone shows a clear signed-in confirmation; return to the computer and it should enter the space automatically.
- Useful on shared or untrusted PCs (for example an internet cafe): the computer never asks for your wallet password.
