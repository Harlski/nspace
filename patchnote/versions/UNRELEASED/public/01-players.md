# Public patch notes — players (`UNRELEASED`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [NEW] **Return Gold** cubes can appear across a wide area around the NimiqLIVE Resident after a Gold Block (or Return Gold) mine, including blocks that are not right next to you. They look like gold and mine the same way.
- [NEW] Mining Return Gold pays **1 NIM**, counts toward your Daily Earn Allowance, and turns that cube back into a normal solid. It may become Return Gold again later.
- [CHANGE] When your Daily Earn is used up, new Return Gold stops appearing until the next UTC day. Cubes already in the world stay claimable.
