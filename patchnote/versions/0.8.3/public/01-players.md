# Public patch notes — players (`0.8.3`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [NEW] **Mosquito Tag achievements** under Minigames: raise a Tag Call, Join one, Pass the mosquito, Pass with 3 seconds or less left, get Stung, pick up a Boost Pad as Holder.
- [FIX] Login streak now counts a day when you are in the world that UTC day, even if your session was still valid from yesterday and you did not sign again.
- [CHANGE] Passing the mosquito flashes the screen green. Getting it still flashes red.
- [CHANGE] When a Tag Round ends, everyone sees the bitten player's identicon next to "{name} got bit by the mosquito".
- [CHANGE] Once Mosquito Tag starts, you cannot take a teleporter or change rooms until the round ends. Screen-edge arrows point toward other players who are off-screen, so portrait play can still find the chase.
- [CHANGE] In Nimiq Pay, Mosquito Tag zooms you out to Telescope range for the countdown and the round, then restores your view. You do not need to hold Telescope or have it unlocked.
- [FIX] Opening Advertise while signed out now says you must be signed in, instead of "Could not load campaigns."
- [CHANGE] New campaign images must be uploaded (PNG, JPEG, or WebP). You can no longer paste an image URL. Existing ads keep showing until you or an operator replace the image.
