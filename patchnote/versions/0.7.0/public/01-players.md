# Public patch notes — players (`0.7.0`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [NEW] **Sale Displays** — stand on the green buy pad (or click while on it) to try and buy a published cosmetic; mannequins can walk a short loop while the pad stays put.
- [NEW] **Shop is open by default** — Wardrobe Shop and Shaper-linked shop flows are available unless operators close them. Buying auto-opens Pay/Hub when needed.
- [NEW] **Player Level** — nameplates show `· Lv N` from Achievement Points; Achievements Summary and profile show your level. Daily Earn Allowance can pause mining payouts when you hit the day cap (toast when it binds).
- [NEW] **Achievements wave** — new Level, Worldcraft, Social, Play Space, Cosmetics, and Exploration milestones; nameplate frame and chat bubble rewards return (`simple` / `neon` frames, pastel / sharp bubbles).
- [CHANGE] Click-to-walk in ordinary rooms is smoother: your avatar follows the accepted path at a steady speed. Soccer free-move is unchanged.
- [CHANGE] Other-player menu is nested: View {username}, Accept 1v1, View Profile / Whisper, and (for admins) More → Administrative. Copy Wallet left this menu.
- [FIX] Changing rooms no longer leaves the camera stuck on the previous room; mid-walk door/teleport should not strand you beside spawn.
- [FIX] Temporarily unavailable cosmetics rows stay honest in Progress Overview when Shop/Shaper is closed.
