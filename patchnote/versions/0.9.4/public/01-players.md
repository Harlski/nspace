# Public patch notes — players (`0.9.4`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

---

- [CHANGE] After you scan a sign-in QR code, the phone shows the same four-character code as the computer, labeled **This screen is**, before you sign.
- [CHANGE] After you walk through a teleporter, **Return to Hub** becomes **Return to last room**. It puts you back on the teleporter you took. If that teleporter has moved, you arrive at the room's spawn instead. If that room is gone, you arrive in the Hub.
- [CHANGE] Player rooms zoom out to the same distance as a normal room, so a very wide floor no longer pulls the camera up to a tiny overview.
- [CHANGE] **Public Rooms** shows a countdown in the hint strip under the title: how long until the room order updates. It resets when the order updates, about every 5 seconds.
