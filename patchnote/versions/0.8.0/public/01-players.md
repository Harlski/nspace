# Public patch notes — players (`0.8.0`)

**Audience:** people who play or explore Nimiq Space — features, fixes, and feel; not implementation detail.  
**Depth:** short bullets or short paragraphs; avoid file paths and internal names unless they help (e.g. a renamed control).

- [NEW] **Language** in the Player Menu — look for the **flag** icon, open the language popup, and pick English, Türkçe, or Português (Brasil). The popup still shows the English word **Language** at the top so you can find it again after switching. Your choice is remembered across visits.
- [NEW] Where a translation is not ready yet, you still see English (not broken labels). More of the UI will follow over time; mining and daily NIM limit messages already follow your language when a translation exists.
